# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in OneVerifyCode to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and constructive when discussing code or product decisions.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the impact of your words.
- Respect privacy. Do not post real phone numbers, SMS bodies, verification codes, or private screenshots.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, privacy constraints, and release timing.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Encouraging others to bypass the app's privacy or read-only safeguards.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block participants who violate this policy. Reports can be sent through the repository's private security reporting channel when privacy is involved, or to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept confidential where possible.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
